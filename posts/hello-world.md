# Why Good Systems Feel Like Magic

Have you ever used a product or service that just *worked*? You didn't have to think about what to do next. The friction was entirely gone. 

This isn't an accident. When systems are designed well, they absorb the complexity so the human doesn't have to. 

## The Anatomy of a Good System

1. **Anticipation**: It knows what you need before you ask.
2. **Forgiveness**: It doesn't break when you make a mistake.
3. **Invisibility**: It stays out of your way.

### Why this matters

We spend so much time building workflows and processes that make sense on a spreadsheet, but fail miserably when real people try to use them.

> "A system is only as good as the human experience it creates."

If we want to build better businesses, we have to start by building better systems—systems that feel like magic.
